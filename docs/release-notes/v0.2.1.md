# vibegraph v0.2.1

A fix release. It makes the Linux release binaries scale with threads, adds
`vibegraph enumerate` and diagram artifacts, and fixes the remaining panics
and silent wrong answers found before release, among them several amplitude
sign errors that MadGraph comparisons on new processes exposed.

## Upgrading

- **Artifacts written by v0.2.0 are refused.** `integrate` artifacts and diagram
  artifacts now record the enumeration that produced them, and a file from
  another enumeration is refused by name. v0.2.1 changes the fermion signs that
  artifacts store, so integrate again from the card.
- **σ moves on the cards named under "Physics fixes".** A result from v0.2.0
  on such a card is wrong; regenerate it.

## Highlights

- **Linux binaries scale with threads.** The static musl builds used musl's
  allocator, which serialises on a lock: `-j 4` ran several times slower than
  `-j 1`. They now use mimalloc and scale like the glibc build (#128).
- **`vibegraph enumerate`** writes a diagram artifact (`diagrams.bin.zst`),
  `diagrams.json` and per-diagram SVGs. `integrate` can start from a diagram
  artifact, and the integrate artifact is self-contained: it embeds the model
  and diagrams, and an amplitude probe refuses a build that no longer
  reproduces it (#114–#121).
- **Configuration shares.** The integrate artifact records each configuration's
  share of σ and each channel's members; `vibegraph inspect --ranking` prints
  the ranking (#124, #126).

## Physics fixes

These change results. Each was a silent wrong answer, and each is now pinned
by a MadGraph comparison that fails without the fix.

- **The relative fermion sign depended on how the card lists its legs.** Two
  pairings of the same fermion lines could interfere with the wrong sign:
  `e+ e- > e- e+` read 8.3% above `e+ e- > e+ e-`. Affected, among others:
  `p p > l+ l- l+ l-` and `p p > e+ e- e+ e-`, `p p > z z, z > l+ l-`,
  `p p > b b~ b b~`, and `p p > t t~ b b~` with electroweak orders, where the
  W-exchange diagrams had the opposite sign to MadGraph's. Pure-QCD `t t~ b b~`,
  `p p > j j` and top pairs were not affected. (#133)
- **SMEFTsim dipole operators** disagreed with MadGraph by up to 1% per colour
  flow (#134):
  - quark–boson dipoles on an incoming or annihilating quark line (ctW, ctB,
    cbW, cbB, cbG), for example `b b~ > t t~` and `b b~ > e+ e-`;
  - the CP-odd W dipoles (ctWIm, cbWIm);
  - ctG with two gluons, `g g > t t~`;
  - ctW and cbW in tW single-top production, `b g > t w-`.
- **`cut_decays = F` with identical particles across a decay** now applies a
  pairing-weighted cut: each pairing of the identical particles exempts its own
  decay products, weighted by its share of |M|². v0.2.0 cut every such leg, which
  read −26% against MadGraph on `g g > z u u~, z > u u~` and −6.8% on
  `g g > z j j, z > j j`; they now read +1.7% and +0.4%, from the interference
  and decay window vibegraph keeps (below). Reweighting such a card is refused,
  because a reweight ratio would leave the pairing weight at the original
  parameters. (#131)

## Panics fixed

- Compiling SMEFTsim `u u~ > t t~ b b~ NP<=1` (a four-quark contact operator)
  panicked. Two causes, both fixed (#132).
- At fixed energy with `sde_strategy = 2`, cards with several subprocesses of
  different diagram content (decay-chain lines forcing different resonances)
  panicked. Each subprocess now has its own channel weights (#132).

## Known issues

### Registered deviations from MadGraph
- **Identical particles across decays.** vibegraph keeps every pairing, their
  interference and the union of their decay windows, where MadGraph keeps one
  pairing with its own window. σ reads +1.7% (`cut_decays = F`) and +1.4% (`T`)
  on `g g > z u u~, z > u u~`, and +0.4% on `g g > z j j, z > j j`. A
  `--madgraph-compat` flag reproducing MadGraph's choice is planned.
- **Widths below 1e-6 of the mass** keep the model's width, where MadEvent
  floors it: 1.4% on one CMS decay-chain card.

### Open
- Reweighting an identical-particle decay chain at fixed energy fails the
  reweighter's own consistency check, so the run is refused, but the message
  names the wrong cause.
- The open measurements of v0.2.0 stand: the `ℓℓj` rows (+0.15% to +0.23%),
  the `b b~` drift with budget, two unattributed heap aborts, the low-budget
  seed spread with one hard cut, and slow multi-jet MLM cards.

### Not supported (refused by name)
Unchanged from v0.2.0: one-body final states, Majorana fermions, the
coloured-scalar seagull, `import model` options, coupling orders without
`coupling_orders.py`, beams other than unpolarized proton–proton or
fixed-energy partons, beam polarization, CKKW-L, photon isolation, NLO, and an
`xqcut` card with `auto_ptj_mjj = F` and no `mmjj` or `drjj`.
