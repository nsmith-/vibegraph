# vibegraph v0.2.0

vibegraph is a leading-order Monte Carlo event generator validated against
MadGraph5_aMC@NLO 3.7.1. v0.2.0 covers LO generation at any leg multiplicity
with MLM merging, MadGraph's process grammar, and proton–proton or
fixed-energy partonic beams. Every card setting it does not honour is refused
by name at parse time; none is silently ignored.

## Highlights

- **MadGraph's process grammar.** Decay chains, `1 → n` decays, s-channel
  restrictions (`$`, `/`, `$$`), polarized legs, `add process`, and
  `import model --modelname` with MadGraph's default particle names.
- **MLM matching** at MadEvent parity, mixed multiplicities included. With no
  run card, a mixed-multiplicity jet process now gets MadGraph's matched
  defaults (`ickkw = 1`, `xqcut = 30`).
- **General UFO models.** Any Lorentz and colour structure, conditional
  parameters, restriction cards and SMEFTsim, gated against MadGraph.
- **Event output.** LHEF in MadGraph's layout, with scale, PDF and αs
  variation weights in MadGraph's systematics format, and MadGraph reweight
  cards at generation.
- **Run-card PDFs.** The run card picks the LHAPDF set and member. CMS's
  880-member systematics request fits in a 2 GB slot.
- **Beams.** Unequal beam energies, and massive incoming partons with
  MadGraph's kinematics. With no run card, lepton beams get MadGraph's
  defaults.
- **CLI.** `integrate --param-card`, `--output-ldjson`, `vibegraph inspect`,
  and a `generate` that runs from the `integrate` artifact alone.
- **Precompiled binaries** for Linux (static musl, with an x86-64-v3 build)
  and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel).

## Changes since v0.1.0

### Physics fixes
- The contact diagram of an all-boson vertex of self-conjugate legs had the
  wrong sign in `g g > alp g`-like processes. Every such vertex is now signed by
  its momentum parity (σ was 4.2× MadGraph on two CMS cards).
- The mirrored beam ordering is evaluated as MadEvent's `IMIRROR = 2`. It was
  under-sampled on cards with mass-split flavour groups, by up to 16%.
- Decay-chain duplicate subprocesses are kept and summed incoherently, as
  MadGraph does.
- Explicit single-ordering initial states (`u u~ > …`) are counted once.
- MLM scales are clustered in the partonic centre of mass.
- A channel's iterations are combined as their plain mean, which removes a
  Neyman-weighting bias on the 2 → 6 rows.

### Coverage
- Flavour groups:
  - a group with mixed outgoing masses is split instead of refused;
  - groups within rounding (1e-9) merge;
  - conjugate colour bases pair under the flow permutation;
  - each group gets its own cut indicator.
- Cuts:
  - MadGraph's HT cuts (`htjmin`, `htjmax`, `ihtmin`, `ihtmax`) are applied;
  - `pt_min_pdg` is applied as MadGraph does;
  - `xpt* ≤ 0` reads as off;
  - `mxx_only_part_antipart` joins the per-PDG cut list.
- With no run card, the default card follows MadGraph's
  `create_default_for_process`: `maxjetflavor` from the beams, MLM on mixed jet
  multiplicities, lepton beams, and `sde_strategy`.
- The `ckm_no_b_mass` restriction (CMS) is bundled.

### Refusals that replace panics or silent wrong answers
- **Uncut divergences.** A card that leaves a massless soft or collinear
  limit uncut is refused, naming the legs and the cut that would regulate it.
  That covers:
  - jets at `ptj = 0` or `drjj = 0`;
  - a photon on a quark or lepton with no ΔR minimum;
  - an emission collinear to a beam with no pT or rapidity cut;
  - decay-mode cards with massless radiation.

  Such a card used to return a finite σ set by how far the sampler reached
  into the pole.
- A card with no lower bound on ŝ (for example `p p > b b~` at `ptb = 0`) is
  refused before integration. MadGraph integrates it behind an implicit
  √ŝ ≥ 2 GeV floor.
- An incoming particle the PDF does not tabulate is refused, where it used to
  panic.
- Also refused, where these used to panic: 2 → 1 processes, Majorana and
  fermion-number-violating processes, and parameters that are missing or
  defined under `if configuration`.
- A panic in a worker thread now ends the run at once with exit code 101 and
  its message.

### Validation
- The banked layer reads 76 MadGraph reference rows (refdata-13): diagram
  counts, per-helicity amplitudes, σ with independent MadEvent seed records,
  and event samples.
- The electroweak `p p > e+ e- j j` row at `ptj = 0` was retired. It diverges
  at tree level, and its earlier agreement with MadGraph was the two samplers'
  pole floors agreeing.

## Known issues

### Registered deviations from MadGraph
- **Identical particles across decays.** vibegraph keeps every pairing and
  their interference, where MadGraph keeps one. σ reads about +0.2% on such
  cards.
- **`cut_decays = F` with an identical particle both in a decay and in the
  core** (the default setting). vibegraph exempts only legs that are decay
  products in every pairing, a stricter cut than MadEvent's. σ is −26% on
  `g g > z u u~, z > u u~` and −6.8% on `g g > z j j, z > j j`. No kinematic
  exemption is IR-safe with every pairing kept; the fix needs a per-pairing
  |M|².
- **Widths below 1e-6 of the mass.** MadEvent evaluates such a propagator at a
  floored width, and vibegraph keeps the model's width. On a decay chain the
  difference is the Breit–Wigner window's share of each line, 1.4% on one CMS
  card.

### Open measurements
- **`ℓℓj` rows** (`p p > e+ e- j` and relatives) read +0.15% to +0.23% above
  MadEvent's seed records, flat in budget.
- **`b b~`** drifts from MadEvent as the budget grows (+2.5σ at 600k points,
  +3.5σ at 1.2M, over 20 seeds). The cause is not found.
- **Heap aborts.** Two aborts are not yet attributed. A `malloc` abort was seen
  once in seven runs under proton sample load and has not been reproduced
  since.
- **VBF_HH** scatters at a low budget: 16% seed spread at `neval 1e4`, against
  about 10% quoted errors. At `1e5` it reads 1.011 ± 0.008 of MadGraph.
- **Low-budget seed spread** is larger than it needs to be when one leg has a
  much harder pT cut than the others (for example `pta 40` with soft jets). σ
  is unbiased at convergence, but a low-budget run can read low with a small
  quoted error.
- **Multi-jet MLM cards** (V/γ/tt̄ + 3–4 jets, QCD 5 jets) are slow: 37 of 199
  CMS gridpacks exceeded a 600 s limit.

### Not supported (refused by name)
- One-body final states (`p p > z'` with no decay), Majorana fermions, the
  coloured-scalar seagull colour structure, and `import model` options.
- Coupling orders when the UFO has no `coupling_orders.py`.
- Beams other than unpolarized proton–proton or fixed-energy partons, beam
  polarization, CKKW-L, photon isolation, and NLO.
- An `xqcut` card with `auto_ptj_mjj = F` and no `mmjj` or `drjj`, because
  MadGraph's kT clustering cut is not implemented.
