Six seeds at 2M × 8 on the `m(gg)` slices 30–50 and 50–100 GeV scatter at
χ²/dof 2.96 and 2.65. Six seeds cannot separate a real under-quoted error from
scatter. Twenty can ([seed sweeps](../../validation/seed-sweeps-and-budget-ladders.md)).
The inclusive σ and the other slices read χ²/dof 0.4–1.0.
