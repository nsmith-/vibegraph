## Scope

Rows: `pp_to_llj_fixed` (`mmll = 50`), `pp_to_jj`, and one MLM row of your
choice (say which and why).

1. **M1 + M2, edge proximity and alignment.** Extend
   `probe_llj_weight_tail_regions` (`validate_hadronic.rs` ~4288, helper ~4302),
   or add a sibling probe beside it.
   - Per point, record each active cut's signed margin in its own variable, and
     for rejected points the cut that failed first.
   - Per channel and `ε ∈ {1%, 5%, 10%}`, give the share of `Σw`, of `Σw²`, of
     the top-0.1% second moment, of the top-20 weights and of the `w_max`
     scan's top weights carried by points within `ε` of each edge.
   - For near-edge points, record every grid coordinate `x_k` and its absolute
     value (`s/s_tot`, `−t/s_tot`, `y`).
   - Classify each edge as fixed in relative coordinates (clusters at
     `x_k ∈ {0, 1}`), fixed in absolute coordinates, conditional on earlier
     coordinates (a D candidate), or none (C).
2. **M4, `(τ, y)` similarity across channels.** From trained grids (the
   integrate artifact's `grid.bin.zst`, or grids the probe trains), the
   pairwise L1 distance between channels' dimension-0 and dimension-1 bin
   densities, and `N_total/N_j` per channel. State what that says for a
   shared outer grid (A1).
3. **M5, rejected against accepted point cost.** Per channel: the
   trained-grid acceptance `Q` and ns/eval for accepted and rejected points
   separately, so the matrix-element share of per-point time is known. Time it
   on a quiet host. A banked run from an earlier session may still be running
   in `/home/user/wt/gridcoords-P1-baseline`
   (`ps -eo pid,args | grep gridcoords-P1-baseline`). Do the timing after it
