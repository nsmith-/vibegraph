## Scope

1. **√s for proton beams** = 2√(E1E2) at the `integrate` and `generate` sites.
   Equal beams must give exactly the old value (`E1 + E2`): state why it is
   bit-identical in floating point, or compute it so that it is, and pin it by
   a test at the beam energies the gates use.
2. **Plumb the beam energies** (E1, E2) into `ProtonIntegrand` through its
   constructors instead of one `sqrt_s_had`, keeping `s_had` where the
   integrand needs it. Keep the change mechanical; if a constructor's callers
   in `integrate.rs`/`generate.rs` multiply, a small struct beats a growing
   argument list.
3. **`build_frames`**: partons at x_i·E_i, boost
   β = (x1E1 − x2E2)/(x1E1 + x2E2) (MadGraph's cm_rap, `genps.f:345`). The
   (τ, y) draw is unchanged. For equal beams the frames must be bit-identical
   to today's: check that the expression reduces exactly, or branch on
   equality, and say which.
4. **Tests**: a unit test at unequal energies (say 6500 × 4000) against hand
   values for the parton momenta, the boost and a lab-frame cut decision; the
   equal-beam bit-identity of √s and of one frame; every existing seeded
   proton test unchanged (`to_bits` where they already pin bits).
5. **Docs**: `runcard.rs` ~:188, `proton.rs` ~:1372 ("√s = E1 + E2"),
   `cli_fixed_energy.rs` ~:7, `cuts.rs` ~:1043-1044 and any other site that
   states E1 + E2.
6. **Do not touch** `vegas.rs`, `budget.rs` or the phase-space maps beyond
   passing the same √s value they read today (they read √s as a bound). If a
   map must change, STOP and report.

- **Background anything expected to exceed ~2 minutes**
  (`run_in_background: true`, output redirected to a log file prefixed with your
  worktree/session name — parallel siblings share one scratchpad and have
  clobbered each other's unprefixed logs). A backgrounded command notifies on

  self-sustaining wake-up rings); draft your notes and report between completion
  notifications instead of idle-waiting. On resume after a stall, first check

  data; spot-check with `git log` / `git diff --stat` instead.

  with `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0 CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=0 CARGO_PROFILE_TEST_DEBUG=0`

  `Bus error` or a truncated log is a kill, not a result: free only your own
