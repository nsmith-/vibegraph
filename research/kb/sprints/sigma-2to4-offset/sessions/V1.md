## Scope

1. **Reproduce both numbers** on the item's reproducer: this side at the
   item's budget over ≥5 seeds, and MadEvent from ≥5 **fresh, independent
   process directories** (diagnostic runs in the scratchpad, `-e madgraph`),
   at the default budget and at a larger one (more points per iteration and
   more iterations). Report MadEvent's seed χ²/dof and its budget shift: if
   MadEvent climbs toward this side with budget, that is evidence on its own.
2. **An estimator whose error needs no adaptation to be honest.** For
   example plain flat phase-space sampling (RAMBO-like or this crate's
   sampler with adaptation frozen after a short warm-up from a uniform grid)
   at enough points to resolve 0.3 % in the tail. Your choice of tool;
   throwaway probe code in the scratchpad is fine. Say what its error does and
   does not cover.
3. **σ in sliced regions on both sides**: at least `m(ll) > 150` and
   `ŝ > 600 GeV` and their complements, imposed as run-card cuts (or
   `dummy_cuts`) on the MadEvent side and as the same cuts here, so each
   region is integrated directly rather than read off an inclusive sample.
   Check that the slices sum to the inclusive result on each side.
4. **Attribute.** Name the side, the region and the mechanism as far as the
   evidence goes (for example, a MadEvent channel that under-samples the tail,
   or an acceptance difference here). Say what would falsify it.

If the evidence puts the defect on this side and the fix is unambiguous and
local, stop and report the fix arithmetic rather than landing it; the manager
dispatches the fix as its own session. If it points into
`vibegraph-lib/src/helas/`, stop and report.
