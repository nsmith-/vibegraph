## Scope

1. **Expose the allocation.** `probe_2to6_budget_ladder` passes
   `BlockAllocation::ByAlpha` to `adapt_grids_budget`. Add a `VG_2TO6_ALLOC`
   knob (`alpha`, `neyman`, or a comma list of both), print it in every
   summary line, and add it as a TSV column. Document it beside the other
   parameters. Also record per seed, for each rung, the number of floor-bound
   channels per iteration and the fraction of the budget above the floors.
2. **Check the premise before the long run.** `allocate_iteration`
   (`vibegraph-lib/src/budget.rs` ~:1001) re-splits only the points above the
   floors, and iteration 0 is the α split under both rules. On two or three
   seeds, dump channel 551's (and 613's) point count per iteration under
   Neyman at 600k and 1.2M. If Neyman leaves them at their floors at both
   rungs, their draws are still shared and the ladder cannot pass; say so and
   stop before the 20-seed run, with the numbers.
3. **The paired ladder.** `bbx` only, multi sampler, 8 iterations, 20 seeds
   (`VG_2TO6_SEEDS=20`, B1's seeds), 600k and 1.2M, under both allocations on
   this tree. B1's main-tree `ByAlpha` TSV is not on this tree; rerun the
   control unless you can show the tree is identical for every file the probe
   compiles. Report per arm and rung: mean, quoted error, per-seed sd,
   χ²/dof, and the sd ratio 1.2M/600k. Read the rung difference as the mean
   and sd of the per-seed paired difference, not `√(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂)`.
4. **The falsifier.** Under Neyman, channel 551's across-seed variance must
   fall with its point count. Measure it at both rungs under both
   allocations, and do the same for channel 613 (seed 55's tail on main).
5. **Cost.** Wall time per seed for each arm, and the σ each arm reaches
   against the 20-seed MadEvent references in the
   [two-to-six-gate log](../../two-to-six-gate/log.md) (reported and
   equal-weight means), read as pulls with the measured spread.
6. **Where the default should change.** If Neyman passes, list every caller
   that fixes `ByAlpha` for a fixed budget (the probe, `integrate_with` in
   `validate_sigma.rs`, the CLI's `fixed_budget` default at
   `vibegraph-cli/src/integrate.rs` ~:464) and the gates a switch would move.
   Do not switch any default: that is D1 for the user.
