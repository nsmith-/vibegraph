## Scope

1. **Reference first.** ≥5 diagnostic MadEvent seeds from fresh directories,
   at the banked budget and at a larger one: is −0.22 % stable on the
   reference side too, or does the reference move?
2. **Where in the scale variable.** σ in slices of the `dynamical_scale_choice
   = 3` scale (or of the parton-level variable it is built from), integrated
   directly on both sides. A uniform ratio points at the coupling's value or
   running (α_s at that scale, the SMEFTsim coupling's scale dependence); a
   localised one points at coverage. Compare also the per-point α_s and
   coupling values this side uses against MadGraph's at the same scale over
   points spanning the cut region, not only kept events.
3. **Control:** the same card at a fixed scale equal to a typical event's
   scale, on both sides — if the offset vanishes, it lives in the running.
4. **Attribute**, with what would falsify it. If the fix is on this side and
   outside `helas/`, give the fix arithmetic, do not land it.
