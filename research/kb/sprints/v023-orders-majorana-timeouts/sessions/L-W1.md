# L-W1: Auto widths

## Context

- Today `DECAY <pdg> Auto` is refused in `ufo/bind.rs` (`CardError::AutoWidth`,
  ~L351). A restrict card with `Auto` is refused separately
  (`UfoError::RestrictAutoWidth`, `ufo/mod.rs` ~L229/L479). Decide whether
  that refusal stays, and say why, citing MadGraph's behaviour.
- PR 8 (#171, merged) added the parameter report: `vibegraph parameters`, and
  `parameters.json` written by `enumerate` (`ufo/report.rs`,
  `vibegraph-cli/src/parameters.rs`, documented in `docs/src/cli/overview.md`
  under "Reporting a model's parameters"). The `Constraint::NotAuto` constraint
  on width parameters (`report.rs` ~L67/L288) is today's refusal and must
  change with it. The computed width, its method and its per-channel partials
  go on the parameter record as an added field. Bump `schema_version` only if
  the change is not purely additive, and say which.
- The widths must also be recorded in the integrate artifact with the card, so
  a rerun of the artifact reproduces them. Choose whether the bound card holds
  the computed number, and state the provenance (the computed width, not the
  literal `Auto`).

## MadGraph semantics first

Before writing code, read and cite (file:line) in the pinned checkout
`research/refs/mg5amcnlo`:
- where MadGraph turns `DECAY ... Auto` into a computation at launch, and
  with which defaults (`compute_widths`, `--body_decay`, `--precision_channel`,
  `--min_br`, and whatever else applies);
- how it builds two-body channels and evaluates partial widths: analytic, or
  through matrix elements; the symmetry factor for identical final states; the
  colour and helicity averages and sums; what happens to closed channels and
  to massless or zero-width daughters;
- whether the computed widths feed back into each other (a width used in
  another width's computation) and in what order;
- what happens beyond two body under the default `body_decay`. The item
  accepts two-body only, and anything more must be **reported as not
  included**, by name, on the record. Where MadGraph's default *would* add a
  three-body correction, state on the record that it is omitted; that is a
  silent-difference risk.
- Any MadWidth option a card or run card can set: match it or refuse it by
  name.

## Refusals

  order cannot be resolved;
- a particle with no open two-body channel (its width is 0, or refuse it:
  check what MG does and cite it);
- a channel needing a loop-level vertex the model lacks.
