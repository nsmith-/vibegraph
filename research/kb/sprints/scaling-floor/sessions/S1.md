## Scope

1. **Design, then size offline.** Today's floor is `floor_for_acceptance`
   (`vibegraph-lib/src/budget.rs` ~:166): `⌈512 / acceptance⌉`, capped at
   4 × 512, independent of `neval`. Propose a floor that grows with
   `neval` (for example a fixed fraction of `neval / n_channels`, or 512
   scaled by `neval` over a reference budget) and keeps at least
   `MIN_CHANNEL_NEVAL` accepted points at the lowest budget any gate uses.
   State, for each candidate: whether total spend stays at `neval` (floors
   taken out of the α shares) or grows past it, what a channel with
   acceptance under 1/4 gets, and whether the count depends on anything the
   run measures. A count read from measured acceptance or variance is
   outcome-dependent; #75 made that safe for the combine but say what it does
   to the stream positions. From the existing traces in the scratchpad
   (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-vibegraph/c98eb2f4-0e99-502e-b316-ad96baf4894f/scratchpad`: `t26-B1-main-trace.txt` ByAlpha and `fbn-P1-neyman-trace.txt`,
   20 seeds × 2 rungs; P1's `fbn-P1-scripts/` read them), compute each
   candidate's per-channel counts at 600k and 1.2M: the floor-bound count,
   the fraction of points above the floors, the points per iteration, and
   how many channels would still draw identical counts at both rungs. Pick
   one and say why.
2. **Implement it as a selectable rule.** Add the scaling floor beside
   today's, chosen by a parameter that defaults to today's rule, so this PR's
   default path is bit-identical. Show that (seeded outputs unchanged on the
   default path). Expose the choice in `probe_2to6_budget_ladder` (a
   `VG_2TO6_FLOOR` knob, recorded in its summary lines and TSV). Test the
   rule's arithmetic and its `MIN_CHANNEL_NEVAL` guarantee.
3. **The paired ladder.** `bbx` only, multi sampler, ByAlpha, 8
   iterations, B1's 20 seeds (`VG_2TO6_SEEDS=20`), 600k and 1.2M, with the
   scaling floor. The control is today's floor on the same tree: B1's
   main-tree TSV and trace are on a tree whose combine differs, so either
   rerun the control or replay it under the plain mean from the trace and
   show the replay exact on two seeds per rung. Report per arm and rung: mean,
   quoted error, per-seed sd, χ²/dof, sd ratio 1.2M/600k, points per
   iteration and wall time per seed. Read the rungs as the mean and sd of the
   per-seed paired difference. Compare the arms at equal drawn points too,
   not only equal `neval`: a floor that adds points must beat today's floor
   given the same spend.
4. **The falsifier.** Channel 551's quoted variance must fall with its point
   count at every seed, not only where a tail point lifted it. Measure it,
   and channel 613, under both arms at both rungs.
5. **What a default switch moves (D1 for the user).** In a throwaway patch,
   or with the parameter set globally, run `pixi run --skip-deps validate`
   (banked layer) with the scaling floor against a baseline on this tree,
   detached, and list every cell that moved, with ≥5 seeds for any enforced
   cell that moved. Also say which long-tier gates (the mixed-row MLM gates,
   the 2 → 6 integrals) and which budgets the switch would touch, and what
   20-seed deciding pairs a switch would need. Do not commit the default
   switch.
