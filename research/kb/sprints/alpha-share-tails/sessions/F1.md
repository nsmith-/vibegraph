## Scope

1. **The symmetric split (default).** Every H → ZZ* channel draws the same Z
   pair first, on its pole, so the half of the Higgs peak where the other
   pair is near shell sits in a Breit–Wigner tail. Make the split symmetric:
   either draw either pair first with probability ½ inside one channel (its
   density the average of the two orderings' densities, so the weight stays
   exact), or add mirror channels. Say which and why (channel count, α
   survey, cost per point). Apply it to every X → VV* split where both
   daughters are drawn as Breit–Wigners, not only H → ZZ*; list the rows and
   channels that change. Pin the density with a test that fails if the two
   orderings are not both counted (e.g. the density integrates to 1, or
   matches a brute-force mixture of the two orderings at sampled points).
2. **The α floor (selectable, default off).** After the α survey, floor
   every α at `1e-4` and renormalise, behind a parameter that defaults to
   today's behaviour. T1 measured `1e-4` as costing about 9% in second
   moment on typical iterations; `1e-3` cost ×2.2. Expose it in the 2 → 6
   ladder probe (`VG_2TO6_ALPHA_FLOOR`) and in `probe_2to6_tail_points`.
3. **Check the arithmetic first.** Before the ladder, rerun T1's seed 55
   point (channel 613, iteration 3, 1.2M) and two others from its Step 5
   table through the implemented code and show their weights match T1's
   counterfactuals (symmetric split ×≈1/197 at seed 55's point).
4. **The ladder.** `bbx`, multi sampler, ByAlpha, 8 iterations, B1's 20
   seeds, 600k and 1.2M, in three arms on this tree: control (no change,
   built once from the base and kept), symmetric split, and symmetric split
   plus α floor. Report per arm and rung: mean, quoted error, per-seed sd,
   χ²/dof, sd ratio and paired difference, points per iteration and wall
   time. Read T1's falsifier (a)–(d) for each fixed arm, with the probe's
   record of 613, 614, 598 and 604 for (a) and (b). If (a) and (b) hold and
   (c) fails, say so: the next step is then the heavy-tail error model, not
   another sampler change.
5. **uux.** Same three ZH channels with the same pair order (T1 Found 3).
   Run `uux` at 600k and 1.2M on 5 seeds per arm (control and split) and
   say whether σ moves and whether its ladder changes.
6. **What the split moves.** It is a default change, so it is statistically
   gated: run `pixi run --skip-deps validate` (banked) detached on the base
   and on the change, and list every cell that moved, with ≥5 seeds for any
   enforced cell that moved and 20+ on any deciding pair a moved gate needs.
   Also name the long-tier gates (2 → 6 integrals, MLM, Higgs-window rows)
   that run an X → VV* split and say whether you ran them. The α floor, being
   off by default, moves nothing; measure what turning it on would move
   (validate with it forced on in a throwaway patch) for the user's D1.
