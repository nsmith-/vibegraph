   uncertainty and the threshold choice stated). An index at or under 2
   means the per-point variance does not exist and `√n` scaling is not
   expected; say which channels are there.
2. **Record the tail points.** Add a test-only (`#[cfg(test)]` in-crate,
   or an `#[ignore]`d probe) record of the largest-weight points per
   channel and iteration: momenta, the channel's own density, every
   channel's density at that point (the multichannel denominator), the
   matrix element, and the cuts. Seed 55's 1.18e-8 pb point in 613 at 1.2M
   is the sharpest case; reproduce it first.
3. **Locate them.** For the recorded points, read the invariants: which
   propagators are near their poles, which particles are soft or collinear,
   which cut boundaries are near. Compare with what channel 613's map (and
   the other heavy channels' maps) are built to flatten. Name the
   mechanism: a peak no channel's map covers, a small multichannel
   denominator (every channel's density low where |M|² is not), a VEGAS
   grid that has not adapted, or something else.
4. **Verify a candidate fix arithmetically** on the dumped points before
   proposing it (AGENTS.md): recompute each tail point's weight under the
   candidate (a channel added or remapped, a different map variable, a
   denominator floor, etc.) and show by how much it falls. State the
   falsifier the fix must pass on a ladder.
