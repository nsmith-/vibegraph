`test_eval_jioxxx` (`helas/eval/run.rs`) checks only the `FFV1` sink, with
massless external fermions. So it passes under a flipped left/right relative
sign (`cl·gL − cr·gR`), and it cannot see that the reference port divides the
longitudinal term by complex `m² − imΓ` where the production kernel, like
ALOHA, divides by real `m²`.

1. Add a per-helicity complex comparison of a mixed-chirality current: the Z
   with `gL ≠ gR`, and massive external fermions so `q·J ≠ 0`. Compare against
   the HELAS reference port (`iovxxx`/`jioxxx`), and where feasible against
   MadGraph, for example through a banked amplitude table that reaches the
   current.
2. **Falsifiers:** the test fails under the sign flip, and it fails under a
   change of the longitudinal denominator on either side (temporary mutations,
   reverted, the failing output quoted).
3. Resolve the denominator difference. The production kernel is what the
   MadGraph gates pin, so either align the reference port with ALOHA and say
   so, or keep it and document the difference as HELAS vs ALOHA, with the
   test's tolerance covering only that. Any production-kernel change is out of
   scope: report it instead.
