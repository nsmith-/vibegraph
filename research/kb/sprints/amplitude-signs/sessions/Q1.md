Item: `same-flavour-four-fermion-diagram-count-ungated` (closed by this PR).
Method: [bit-exact amplitude debugging](../../../validation/bit-exact-amplitude-debugging.md);
design of the pairings in note 35 §F1 (`research/kb/history/notes/35-ufo-lorentz-sprint-plan.md`).

`e+ e- > e+ e- NP<=1` with an `O_ll`-type contact: we draw one diagram per
fermion pairing, and MadGraph draws one per contact. This is the first row where
the relative Fermi sign between the two pairings of one contact reaches |M|².

1. Add an `ee_to_ee_4f` standalone row (SMEFTsim, the `vg_c4l` restrict card
   or a justified variant), zero widths if a spacelike Z/γ needs it. Bank it.
   Count the diagrams on both sides by machine.
2. Compare per helicity (|M|² and JAMP) through `standalone_jamps`, informational
   first. Since the diagram lists differ, compare per diagram by summing our
   pairings of one contact against MadGraph's one contact diagram (its `AMP()`
   from `--amps`). Report the per-pairing relative sign.
3. **Falsifier:** flip the relative sign between the two pairings (temporary
   mutation) and show the comparison fails.
4. If it agrees, flip the row to gating. Record the diagram-count difference as a
   convention cell, the way `gg_to_gg_cg`'s is recorded. A convention cell is a
   decision, so draft its wording for the manager, who takes it to the user. If
   it disagrees, keep it informational, localise, and report. Do not fix.

Gate: `cargo test --workspace`, fmt, clippy, `pixi run --skip-deps validate`.
