## The rule

Per channel tested (every seed at the budget carries it with a nonzero
error): χ² = Σ((xᵢ − x̄)/eᵢ)² about the unweighted mean, on n − 1 dof, and its
p-value Q((n − 1)/2, χ²/2). With N channels tested:

- **flag** when N · p(worst channel) < 0.01 (look-elsewhere corrected);
- **confirmed** when the same test holds on a row with at least 20 seeds at
  the budget; at 5–19 seeds a flag reads "confirm with 20 seeds";
- **for information only**, as today: the count above χ²/dof 3 and the count
  expected there by chance.

The worst channel is the one with the smallest p-value (with equal seed
counts per row that is the largest χ²).

## Scope

1. Implement the rule in the shared reader (`tests/common/seed_record.rs`)
   and the `SCATTER` screen line, which states the corrected p-value, the
   channel count, the verdict (`within chance`, `FLAGGED: confirm with 20
   seeds`, `CONFIRMED`), and the informational count.
2. **Records already being written stay readable.** The user is banking
   now with the driver at `02e2a28` (schema 2). A schema-2 record from that
   driver must read with the new code and give the new verdict, derived from
   the per-seed `by_channel` values it holds. Keep the schema at 2 unless a
   stored field's meaning changes; if it must change, say why in the report
   and make the reader accept the batch's records. Show it on F2's dry-run
   records (`scratchpad/refscf2/dry/`).
3. **Tests with mutation evidence:** the corrected p-value against a
   known-answer value (e.g. χ² = 12 on 4 dof → 7·e⁻⁶; N = 6 scales it by 6);
   a row of many channels quoting their errors correctly does not flag; one
   channel far outside its quote flags; 20 seeds confirm; the threshold side
   of 0.01. Each test must fail under a mutation that breaks what it pins.
4. **Measured check:** run the rule over the kept `hard_survey = 2` seeds
   (`scratchpad/refscf2/hs2x/keep`, rows ee, uux, llj; the manager's
   measurement scripts are `scratchpad/f3rule/rules.py` and `confirm.py`)
   and reproduce, within subset noise, the 5-seed fire rates uux 92 %,
   `ee_to_mumua` 21 %, llj 0 %, and the all-seed look-elsewhere p for
   `ee_to_mumua` ≈ 1.6·10⁻⁷. The Rust and the Python must agree on the
   full-sample p-values.
