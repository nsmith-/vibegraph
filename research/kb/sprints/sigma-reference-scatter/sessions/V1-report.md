Phase A (MadEvent side, steps 1–3) is complete. Phase B is the CM-clustering
fix, measured as an uncommitted probe and reverted. No source change is
committed.

## Method

- **Fresh directories.** One process directory per row, generated by the
  pinned 3.7.1 and never run. Each seed copies it to a new directory, installs
  the committed run card with `iseed` set, runs `generate_events` once on
  2 cores, and is then deleted. A copy of a never-run directory has no grids,
  so this is the same as `FRESH_ROWS` regenerating per seed.
- **Card check.** Every kept banner's `<MGRunCard>` was checked against the
  card it was given: 0 mismatches over all 85 runs (`drjl` 0.4 → 0.0 being
  the `xqcut` rewrite). On one run per row, the banner was also diffed against
  the banked run. Only `iseed` differs, plus `pdlabel`'s whitespace on the
  scatter rows.
- **Reproduction controls.** A fresh directory at the bank's own seed
  reproduces the bank to every printed digit:
  - `pp_to_llj_mlm` at 20260928: 267.66 ± 0.89073, equal to
    `mlm_sigma_reference.json`'s first run.
  - `dy13_mmll_60_120` at 33: 644.33 ± 0.28345.
  - `pp_to_bb_fixed` at 21: 2143300 against 2143300.7.
  - `pp_to_ll_scalefact2` at 21: 1958.9 against 1958.95.
  - `pp_to_llj_dyn` at 21: 415.42 against 415.4176.
  - `pp_to_bb` at 21: 376240000 against 376243100.

  So this host and procedure are MadEvent's bank procedure, and the seed is
  the only variable.
- Kept per run: `results.dat`, the banner, every `P*/G*/results.dat`, the
  log. Tarball: `refsc-work/refsc-phaseA-keep.tgz` in the scratchpad.
- **Equal-weight recombination (EW).** Per channel, the unweighted mean of the
  iterations in its `G*/results.dat`, summed over channels. The same
  definition as `iter_seed.py`.
- **Seed means.** These follow the seed policy: unweighted mean, error
  max(√Σs²/n, sd/√n). χ²/dof is taken about the mean, using each seed's own
  quoted error.

Two sensitivity checks on EW:
- Dropping each channel's first one or two iterations moves the paired shift
  by at most 0.03 %: +0.20 %, +0.19–0.21 %, +0.24–0.26 %. The shift does not
  come from unadapted early iterations.
- An inverse-variance recombination of the same iterations reproduces the
  reported value within 0.15 % per seed, so the reading of the files is right.

### What step 1 says

- **Fresh directories do not move the llj references.** Each 20-seed fresh
  mean sits within 0.75σ of its shared-directory bank.
- **The offset is not a shared-directory artefact.** The ours-high offset of
  the matched rows survives against fresh seeds (+0.14 %, +0.18 %), and
  `xqcut_only` reads −0.08 %. The rows that carry `rewgt` sit high and the
  pure-cut row does not. Phase B tested the clustering frame against that
  pattern, and it does not explain it.
- **Fresh seeds also scatter below their quoted errors.** χ²/dof is 0.55,
  0.53 and 0.58 over 19 dof, each near the lower 5 % point
  (p = 0.06 / 0.05 / 0.08), and 0.55 pooled over 57 dof (p = 0.002). The
  shared-directory banks read 0.32–0.56. Directory sharing therefore
  explains little of the low χ²/dof: MadEvent's quoted per-run error on these
  rows is itself about 1.35× its seed scatter.
- **The iteration-weighting shift is +0.18 % to +0.23 %**, about the fixed-beam
  2 → 4's +0.18 %, and 3.8σ to 6.5σ on every row. The mechanism is visible in
  the files. For example, seed 30261110, `P1_qq_llg/G2`: 21 refine
  iterations of about 153 pb, one of which reads 176.6 ± 23.0. That iteration
  counts once in EW and almost not at all in mean²/variance.
- **EW does not land on this side everywhere.** On the two `rewgt` rows EW
  removes the offset. On `xqcut_only` it opens one (−0.26 %, −2.5σ). EW is
  exposed to optional stopping: the number of refine iterations per channel
  (3 to 21) depends on the precision reached. It needs an adaptation-free
  check before D1 makes it a reference.

### What step 2 says

- **Each bank sits within 1.1σ of its row's fresh mean.** No single-run
  reference is an outlier draw.
- **The quoted error is not a uniform proxy for seed scatter.** Seed sd over
  mean quoted error runs from 0.43 to 1.62 across five rows: χ²/dof 0.19 to
  2.67. Two rows fall outside the central 95 %, one at each end.
  - `dy13_mmll_60_120`'s seeds scatter 1.6× beyond the quote, and its 4×
    run lands 2.4σ above the 6-draw mean.
  - `pp_to_ll_scalefact2`'s and `pp_to_bb`'s scatter at about half the quote.

  So a gate on max(quoted, spread/√n) over one draw would understate
  `dy13`'s MadEvent error by about 1.6×. On the rows where the quote is too
  large, the gate is merely loose.
- **One 4× run per row cannot separate a budget shift from scatter.** The
  4× − 1× differences are −0.25 % to +0.08 %, within 2.4σ on every row, with
  no common sign. Note that `dy13`'s 1× mean errs by the quote while its
  spread says 1.6× more. A budget-shift claim needs several 4× seeds against
  the measured 1× spread, which the AGENTS ladder rule asks for.
- **The EW shift is smaller on these rows**, +0.03 % to +0.17 % (paired), and
  significant only on `dy13` (+0.10 %, 3.6σ). The llj MLM rows' +0.2 % is the
  larger end. The size tracks how many refine iterations a channel takes.

## Step 3: re-bank cost

- **Per-run cost.** One 10 k-event hadronic run costs 30–56 s on 2 cores
  here. The banked M0 runs on the M3 took 18–39 s for llj, against 29–49 s
  here: the M3 is about 0.7× this host per run.
- **Per-row cost.** A row re-banked at 5 seeds + two 4× seeds costs
  5 + 2 × 2.6 ≈ 10 single-run units. That is about 8 min here, or about
  5.5 min on the M3, per 10 k-event row. A `dy13` row (200 k events, 4× at
  4.1×) costs 5 × 89 s + 2 × 365 s ≈ 20 min here, about 14 min on the M3.
- **Seed count.** At 20 seeds per row instead of 5 (the ladder rule, for a
  row whose budget rungs are to be compared), the 1× part scales by 4.
- **Parallelism.** Runs of different rows are independent. The M3's core
  count divided by `nb_core` sets the concurrency.

## Phase B: the CM-clustering fix on the llj MLM rows

Commit `6740954` was applied to `d51304d` as an uncommitted probe
(`cherry-pick -n`). The `validate_hadronic` test binary was built at
`release-debug` with debuginfo off (`--config profile.release-debug.debug=0`)
to keep it under the disk floor. Debuginfo does not change codegen. The
three gates were run with their own seeds at 150000 × 10.

The probe was then reverted (`git reset` and `git checkout` on
`vibegraph-lib`), and the same three gates were rerun on the same build
settings for a paired baseline. The baseline reproduces the 2026-10-14
manifest readings to every printed digit: 212.6082 ± 0.1259, 268.4331 ±
0.1193, 241.2550 ± 0.1535.

- `mlm`: +0.13 %, +1.54σ.
- `alps2`: +0.17 %, +1.78σ.

### What Phase B says

- **The clustering frame is not the llj offset.** The fix moves the two
  `rewgt` rows down by 0.006–0.007 %, a twentieth of their +0.14 % and
  +0.18 % against MadEvent. It leaves the pure-cut row unchanged on every
  seed.
- **This falsifies the step-1 reading** that the `rewgt`-rows-high pattern
  fits a lab/CM scale difference, at least at this size.
- **Why the pure-cut row cannot move.** In `p p → e+ e- j` the only jet
  vertex is initial-state, whose kt is the jet's pT, boost-invariant. So the
  `xqcut` decision and the clustering scale cannot change. Only `rewgt`'s
  beam-side assignment can.
- **The fix should still land.** It is MadEvent's frame (its own commit
  argues that from `cluster.f`). This row set just cannot see it beyond
  0.01 %.
- **What does fit the step-1 pattern is the reference estimator.** Equal-
  weight recombination of MadEvent's iterations lands on this side for both
  `rewgt` rows (−0.07 %, −0.05 %), while the pure-cut row then reads −0.26 %.
  With every reading inside 2.5σ, the llj rows alone cannot decide between
  an estimator bias on MadEvent's side and a residual in `rewgt`. A per-event
  `rewgt` check already agrees at 1e-12 on MadEvent's written events, so a
  `rewgt` residual would have to live where no written event samples it.

Cost here: build 4 m 46 s (802 MB `target/` without debuginfo), then 3.8,
12.3 and 6.1 min for the three gates on 2 threads.

## Brief errors

- **`gen_hadronic_sigma.sh` banks only two rows**, `dy13_default` and
  `dy13_mmll_60_120` (200 k events, iseed 33). The other gated hadronic rows
  (`pp_to_jj`, `pp_to_bb*`, `pp_to_ll_scalefact2`, `pp_to_llj*`) are
  single runs from `scripts/<row>.mg5` through the build. Step 2 should read
  "single-run hadronic rows".
- **D1's statement** that "seeds sharing a directory hide the bias further,
  because they inherit its grid (χ²/dof 0.3–0.8 on the MLM rows)" is not
  supported. Fresh directories read 0.53–0.58 over 20 seeds. The backlog
  item `reference-sigma-seed-scatter-untested` makes the same attribution.
- The backlog item `llj-mlm-rows-sit-high-together` quotes MadEvent values
  (212.549, 268.169, 240.710) that differ from the current
  `mlm_sigma_reference.json` and the manifest (212.572, 268.147, 240.717).
- The suggested probe revert `git checkout -- .` leaves a `cherry-pick -n`
  staged. The probe paths need `git reset` first.

## Found

- **Every single-run hadronic bank uses one seed, iseed 21.** It is the auto
  seed of a fresh directory, and it appears in the banners of `pp_to_bb`,
  `_fixed`, `_qcd2`, `pp_to_ll_scalefact2`, `pp_to_llj`, `_dyn`, `_fixed` and
  `pp_to_jj`. `gg_to_gg_cg`'s bank has it too. No row has a second draw. A
  re-bank must name its seeds explicitly.
- **MadEvent's quoted error is not its seed scatter, in both directions.** On
  the llj MLM rows it is too large by about 1.35× (pooled χ²/dof 0.55 over
  57 dof). On `pp_to_ll_scalefact2` and `pp_to_bb` it is about 2× too large.
  On `dy13_mmll_60_120` it is 1.6× too small (χ²/dof 2.67, p = 0.02 over
  5 dof). The `dy13` reading needs more seeds to confirm, since 5 dof is
  thin. A reference error built from one run's quote is therefore unreliable
  both ways. The rows where it is too small make a gate tighter than it
  should be.
- **EW against this side on `pp_to_llj_xqcut_only`: −0.26 %, −2.5σ**, while
  EW removes the offset on the two `rewgt` rows. It needs an adaptation-free
  estimator before D1 adopts EW as a reference.
