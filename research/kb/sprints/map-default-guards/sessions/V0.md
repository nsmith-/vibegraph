## Part A — `pp_to_jj` flavour composition

The cell compares each of three generation seeds (off one frozen grid) with
**one** banked MadGraph run of 10 000 events, and gates on the minimum p over
rows × seeds × columns against `1e-4`. Under `--map-tau inverse-square` one
seed reads p 2.8e-5 (128.1/70); the three-seed χ² sum reads 325/212 under τ and
267/210 under log. The three comparisons share the reference sample, so their
sum is not χ² on 210 dof.

1. Under each map (`log`, `inverse-square`), record the per-seed flavour χ²
   and p on the gate's own configuration, and the **pooled** statistic: all
   generation seeds' events in one sample against the reference, one χ².
   Do it at 3 and at 5 generation seeds (`HEADROOM_GEN_SEEDS`).
2. **The null.** How does the statistic read when the sides cannot differ?
   (a) this side against itself, at the gate's sample sizes (20 000 vs 10 000,
   two independent integration seeds, same map); (b) if MadGraph's banked jj
   events can be split, reference half against reference half. Report how far
   the per-seed and pooled statistics sit above their nominal χ² under each
   null, and the trial count the `1e-4` floor is designed against.
3. Independent grids: repeat (1) with a second integration seed per map, so
   the frozen grid's own draw is not mistaken for the map's.
