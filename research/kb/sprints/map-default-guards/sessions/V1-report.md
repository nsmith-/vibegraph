  - `the_rule_reads_the_whole_state_resonance_and_shapes_every_split` pins

    `soft_split_angles_off_is_bit_identical_and_the_root_is_isotropic_either_way`

  diagnosed before it was changed:
  - `pairs_with_equal_map_identities_draw_and_weigh_every_point_alike`:
    `p p > e+ e- j j` has 30 map identities, not 36. A throwaway census (not
    committed) counted 336 pairs under each map: **legacy 30, default 30,
    soft-all + log 30, soft-emission 36**. The 36 was soft-emission's census,

  - `a_merged_channel_is_the_sum_of_the_unmerged_terms_it_replaces`: 253

  - `an_accepted_point_reconstructs_the_value_it_was_drawn_at`: 27 points kept
    in 400 trials, against a floor of 30. The trials went to 800, which keeps
    57; lab balance 4.39e-13 and partonic invariant 4.44e-16, both inside their
    1e-10 and 1e-12 bounds.

  The `1/τ²` draw crowds `ŝ_min`, where llj's cuts reject more. These are
  coverage floors, so more draws keep each test's statement intact.

    `llj_scatter_calibration(run, shape, seeds, label, maps)`.

  - New `_legacy` arms for dyn and fixed, and

`whole_state_resonance` over every diagram of the process. MadEvent decides per
channel. So `p p > b b~ QCD=2`, whose electroweak diagrams carry `Z`/`h`
propagators spanning the pair, stays on `Log` throughout:
- `pp_to_bb_fixed` and `pp_to_bb_qcd2` are bit-identical before and after,
  per the report diff below.
- `pp_to_bb` (`p p > b b~`, QCD only) moves.
- note 37's 0.65 ratio was measured on the QCD-only card.

The same OR in `union_shape` keeps a Drell–Yan part's `Log` for every
multiplicity of a mixed card.

**Re-measured numbers in the doc comment.** The gate readings are this

## Found

- **The τ rule reads the whole process, MadEvent reads the channel.** What is
  wrong: `ProcessShape::whole_state_resonance` is an OR over every diagram, and
  `union_shape` ORs it again over multiplicities. A process with one electroweak
  s-channel diagram among QCD ones keeps the log map on every channel. Evidence:
  `pp_to_bb_fixed` and `pp_to_bb_qcd2` (`p p > b b~ QCD=2`) are bit-identical
  before and after the flip, while `pp_to_bb` (`p p > b b~`) moved; note 37's
  0.65 gain was measured on the QCD-only card. What would close it: a
  per-channel `τ` map (each channel's own resonance decides). That needs the
  mixture's `τ` draw to be per channel, plus a measurement on `p p > b b~
  QCD=2` and on an MLM card with a Drell–Yan part.
- **The shipped llj arm (soft-all + `1/τ²`) has no 80-seed dyn calibration.**
  Evidence: only the gate quintet (1.03) exists for it. V0's two arms both used
  log τ. What would close it: `probe_llj_dyn_scatter_guard_calibration` (now the
  shipped arm) run on this branch, about 96 min at 72 s a seed, read with
  `mapguards-V0-scatter.py`.
