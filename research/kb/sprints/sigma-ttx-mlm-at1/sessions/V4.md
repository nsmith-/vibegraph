## Scope

1. **An adaptation-free estimate** of that σ: fixed-density sampling, never
   adapted, calling MadGraph's standalone `SMATRIX` for each gq subprocess
   with LHAPDF densities. Its error must be honest (the seed spread over ≥ 5
   seeds). Check its phase-space volume against a known value. Compare it with
   vibegraph (118.494 / 137.903 at dynamical / fixed μ in V3) and with
   MadEvent's reported and equal-weight values.
2. **If MadEvent is the outlier:** name the channels it under-integrates (seed
   scatter per `G*`, iteration pattern), as #28 did. Stop there.
3. **If vibegraph is the outlier:** compare per point on vibegraph-drawn gq
   points against V2's `ptdriver_gq.f`, factor by factor. vibegraph's
   `generate` refuses this card (V2's Found 2), so draw the points with a
   probe test instead; throwaway, uncommitted. Name the factor that differs and
   stop; the fix is its own session.
4. **Either way:** say what the post-fix `@1` reads against the reference
   once its gq part is corrected, i.e. whether the `pp_to_ttx_0j1j_mlm`
   integrals cell can gate (|pull| < 3) and against which reference.
