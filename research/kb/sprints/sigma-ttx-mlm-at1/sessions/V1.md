## Scope

1. **Independent reference seeds.** Nine of the banked ten MadEvent seeds share
   one directory. Run ≥5 diagnostic MadEvent seeds of the row's card, each in
   a **fresh** process directory (the generator's `FRESH_ROWS` mechanism is
   the model), and report `@0` and `@1` with their spread and χ²/dof. Budget
   to the host: if a full-budget run is too slow here, measure one run's wall
   time first and choose the largest budget that fits, saying so.
2. **H1 at σ level.** The same, with the first-call patch applied. The
   difference between 1 and 2 is H1's share of `@1` at σ level.
3. **H1 per event.** Measure the share of `@1`'s σ carried by points that only
   one side's first `setclscales` call rejects (rejected points never reach the
   dump, so this needs a replay of the first call on both momenta orderings,
   not the dump). Say which tool did it and what it cannot see.
4. **Reconcile:** does fresh-directory + patched MadEvent close the +1.28 %?
   If a residual remains, localise it (subprocess group, `xqcut` side, jet pT)
   from the per-group σ both sides report, without touching `helas/`.
