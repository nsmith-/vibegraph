## Scope

1. **Fixed-point entry on this side.** Evaluate the matched group term (the
   `per_group_sum` path, with `rewgt`, the member average and the configuration
   draw as the integrand runs them) at given `x1`, `x2`, momenta and
   configuration. Expose each factor separately: PDFs at each beam's scale,
   the α_s factors, the Sudakov/`rewgt` ratio, the flavour-member weights, and
   the channel weight.
2. **Instrumented MadEvent.** A driver over the pinned 3.7.1 `P1_gg_ttxg` (then
   `P1_gq_ttxq`) that calls `setrun`, `setpara` and `setcuts`, then `PASSCUTS`,
   `UPDATE_SCALE_COUPLING` and `DSIG1` with `SUBDIAG(1) = 0`, and prints the
   same factors.
3. **Points.** Draw them from this side's `gg` events with x_max > 0.3, where
   V1 located the excess, and add a control set at small x_max. Compare factor
   by factor. The first factor that differs is the finding.
4. **The `gq` beam-scale reading.** At the `gq` points, check V1's Found 4:
   `auto_dsig1.f` tests `Q2FACT(IB(1))` but reads `Q2FACT(1)`. Does MadEvent
   evaluate a density at the other beam's scale in the mirrored ordering, and
   what is that worth in σ? If it is MadEvent's defect, record it. Do not
   "fix" it on our side.
5. **Fix arithmetic, not a fix.** If the differing factor is this side's,
   show (on the dumped points) that the corrected factor closes the per-point
   ratio, and estimate the σ shift. The manager dispatches the fix as its own
   session.
