## Scope

1. The fixed-beam σ gate integrates each of its 34 rows over `HEADROOM_SEEDS`
   (the gate's seed first), combines them under the seed policy (unweighted
   mean, max(quoted, spread/√n)), and reads the pull and rel against the row's
   seed record exactly as today. `rel_tol`, `|pull| < 3` and every other limit
   are unchanged. A row that newly fails is **not** fixed or loosened: it goes
   `info` with its measurement and a **Found** entry, and you stop and report
   if more than one does.
2. The gate also prints each row's five-seed χ²/dof. Assert nothing new on it.
   It is information for the scatter guard question.
3. `probe_gate_row_seed_headroom` prints rel and pull against the old
   single-run `sigma_reference.json`, not the seed records (V1 Found: it
   prints `ee_to_ttx` at +0.96 where the gate reads +1.21). Make it read the
   reference through the gate's own reader, so the calibration re-record that
   follows uses the gate's reference.
4. Update the gate's doc comments that state its seed count. Leave the
   per-row calibration figures alone: PR #45 re-records them from one run
   after this lands.
5. Record the run: the full gate on the final commit, with every row's
   five-seed reading (mean, error, χ²/dof, rel, pull) and wall time. Then
   re-read the population with `sigma-pulls`, giving the gate's own mean pull
   over five seeds.
