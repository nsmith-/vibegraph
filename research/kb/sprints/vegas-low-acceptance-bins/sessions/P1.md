## Scope

1. **Plumbing first, provably inert.** A grid-rule option (library enum plus a
   CLI flag) whose default is today's rule. Every seeded VEGAS golden and
   integration stays bit-identical (`to_bits` equality of the seeded goldens,
   and a before/after `vibegraph integrate` on two rows comparing result and
   grid hash). Commit this step on its own.
2. **Three rules behind the option:** (a) MadEvent's per-bin inverse-acceptance
   rescale (cap 10⁴); (b) adaptation on Σ|w| instead of Σ(f·w)²; (c) a
   bin-width bound. Each is cited to its source and unit-tested on a toy
   integrand with a known low-acceptance region.
3. **Measure** each rule against today's on the banked rows where the tail
   lives (`pp_to_llj_mlm` and its matched siblings, `pp_to_llj`, `pp_to_jj`)
   and at least two fixed-beam rows as controls: ≥5 seeds per arm, σ (unweighted
   mean, spread, χ²/dof, pull against the seed record), ε_unw, the Hill index
   of the weight tail, and evaluations to target. Read arm differences against
   the measured seed spread; any arm-to-arm claim that turns on a small
   difference gets 20 seeds (AGENTS.md).
4. **Do not change the default.** If a rule is a clear win, write the proposal
   with its table for the manager: a default change moves every integration
   and every σ calibration, so it is the user's decision and is sequenced
   before the calibration re-record (PR #45).
