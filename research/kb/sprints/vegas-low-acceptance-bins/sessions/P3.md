## Scope

1. **The flip.** `integrate`'s default grid rule bounds every bin at K = 6
   times the uniform width. Keep the change at the one place the default is
   decided; `generate` and any other consumer read the rule from the artifact,
   not from their own default.
2. **(a) A way back.** The CLI must be able to select the unbounded grid
   (e.g. `--grid-max-bin-width none`; your call on the spelling, keep the
   existing `K > 1` validation for numbers). Regenerate
   `docs/src/cli/reference.md` with `scripts/gen-cli-docs.sh`.
3. **(b) The grid rule in the artifact.** Record the rule the grids were
   trained under (bin weight, acceptance rescale, bin-width bound) in
   `IntegrateArtifact`, minimally, and forward-compatible with the backlog
   item `artifact-has-no-sampler-kind` (from #56: a later sampler-kind tag
   must be addable beside it without another reshuffle; read that item).
   Bump the format version by the convention of
   `IntegrateArtifact::version_for` (`vibegraph-lib/src/artifact.rs`); older
   artifacts must still load, reading as the rule they were trained under
   (unbounded). Pin both with tests.
4. **(c) One-sitting timing.** K = 6 against base, interleaved in one sitting
   (`research/kb/workflow/end-to-end-timing-protocol.md` §1): 20 seeds on
   `pp_to_llj`, `pp_to_llj_mlm`, `pp_to_jj` at the gates' budgets (P2's
   driver), about 2 h, detached. Report var·CPU K6/base per row with its
   seed-spread error. Until this lands, no variance-per-CPU claim goes in any
   doc or comment.
5. **Banked layer on the combined tree.** PR #51 (`map-default-guards`) flips
   the map defaults; the user wants #45's re-record to happen once, on both
   flips. After your flip commits and the hermetic gates pass, make a
   throwaway local branch `p3-combined` in this worktree that merges
   `origin/map-default-guards` into your tip (do not push it), run
   `pixi run --skip-deps validate` there detached, then switch back to
   `vegas-low-acceptance-bins`. Compare against #51's own banked run on the
   map flip alone (`mapguards-V1-report-after/` in the scratchpad, collator
   `report.json`; `mapguards-V1-diff.py` diffs two reports): list every
   moved cell with before/after. A cell that leaves its limit becomes a
