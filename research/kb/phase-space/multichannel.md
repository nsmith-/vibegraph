## The estimator

A `MultiChannel` combines a fixed set of channels on one `√ŝ` and mass set
(`phasespace/channel.rs`)[^channel-rs]. Channel `j` has selection weight
`αⱼ > 0`, `Σαⱼ = 1`. A point generated by any channel is weighted by

```text
w(p) = 1/g(p),   g(p) = Σⱼ αⱼ gⱼ(p)
```

with every channel's density `gⱼ` evaluated at the *same* point, which is why
a channel must report its density at configurations it did not generate
([channel contract](channel-contract.md)). `g` is the pushforward density of
select-then-draw, so `w·f` is unbiased for `∫dΦ f` at any fixed `α`, and a
point near one channel's peak is not over-counted by the others. Production
integrates the same estimator split into one term per channel, each on its own
grid ([per-channel grids](per-channel-vegas-grids.md)); the mixture form with
a selection coordinate `u[0]` (`select_channel`, by cumulative `α`) remains
for the α survey and as a comparison arm.

Which channels exist — one per diagram, MadGraph's merged configurations,
merged again by map identity — is [the channel set](channel-set.md); how each
channel maps its diagram is [diagram channels](diagram-channels.md).

## α-adaptation (Kleiss–Pittau)

Before the grids are trained, a survey→refine loop drives `α` toward the
variance-minimising mixture[^kp]. Each survey draws `n_survey` points from the
current mixture and estimates every channel's share of the estimator variance,

```text
Wⱼ = E_g[(f/g)²·gⱼ/g]
```

(each point informs every channel), then reallocates with
`kleiss_pittau_step` (`channel.rs:265`):

```text
αⱼ ← αⱼ·Wⱼ^β / Σₖ αₖ·Wₖ^β,   β = ½
```

with every weight floored at `1e-12` of the sum before renormalising, so one
under-sampled survey never kills a channel. A survey that finds no variance
leaves `α` alone and stops. The fixed point is `Wⱼ = const` over channels with
weight, the stationarity condition of `∫f²/g` under `Σαⱼ = 1`: a converged `α`
equalises the per-channel variance shares. The surveyed integrand is evaluated
at the drawn point *and* the drawing channel, because a per-event scale may
read the channel. After the survey `α` is frozen; VEGAS then refines each
channel's hypercube.

- **Fixed beams** (`FixedBeamIntegrand::use_multichannel`): the survey runs
  inside `MultiChannel::adapt_alphas` on the process's `Σ|M|²` with the cut
  applied; constant prefactors are left out because they scale every `Wⱼ`
  equally.
- **Hadronic** (`ProtonIntegrand::adapt_alphas`): the same loop driven from
  outside the combiner, jointly over the `(group, diagram)` channel space,
  because the integrand owns the `(τ, y)` coordinates and therefore the energy
  of each draw. The surveyed `f` is the whole hadronic shape — Jacobian, flux,
  cut and luminosity-weighted group sum — so weight flows to channels carrying
  variance of the *hadronic* integral[^proton-alpha]
  ([τ, y sampling](hadronic-tau-y-sampling.md)). On `p p > l+ l- j` the
  weights spread by 81.9× over 6 surveys of 4 000 points, and the mixture's
  standard error at 30 000 draws fell to 0.77× the uniform mixture's with the
  integral unmoved[^n24-alpha].
- **Generation** replays the artifact's `α` (`use_multichannel_with_alphas`)
  instead of re-surveying, since `αⱼ` enters each channel's weight.

The CLI sizes the survey as `neval.clamp(10 000, 40 000)` points over
`ADAPT_ITERS = 6` surveys (`vibegraph-cli/src/integrate.rs:97-107`)[^integrate-rs].
Measured over `n_survey ∈ {10k, 40k, 160k, 640k}` with the survey seed swept,
σ and `α` are stable from 40k up and 10k is genuinely worse; on wide splits
`α` is iteration-limited (independent surveys land 0.15–0.74 apart in L1 at
every budget, with the six iterations binding, σ indifferent):[^n34-s1]
[α survey iteration-limited](../backlog/performance/alpha-survey-iteration-limited-on-wide-splits.md),
[survey bounds untested at hundreds of channels](../backlog/performance/adapt-survey-bounds-untested-at-hundreds-of-channels.md),
[alternating α and grid training](../backlog/feature/alpha-grid-alternation-unstudied.md).

Two measurement rules came out of this:

- **Measure α's effect on the mixture estimator, not through the per-channel
  grids.** The per-channel budget allocates under a coverage floor, so a
  uniform-α and an adapted run through `adapt_grids` give different point
  counts and conditional densities per grid and are not budget-matched; one
  such comparison read the adapted run as noisier[^n24-alpha].
- **The survey's own byte identity.** Each surveyed point evaluates the
  mixture density once and records every `gⱼ` during that sweep, feeding both
  the weight and the `Wⱼ` row; recovering `g` from the returned weight
  (`αⱼ/(αⱼ/g)`) would re-round it. Artifacts stayed byte-identical[^n34-s1].

The survey's cost is a serial or chunked pass whose length does not scale with
the integration budget, so it appears as the Amdahl term when `-j` wall times
are fitted to a serial-plus-parallel model. The hadronic survey
(`survey_variance`) runs in fixed-size chunks in parallel, its sums reduced in
chunk order so the thread count does not change the answer; the fixed-energy
survey in `MultiChannel::adapt_alphas` is a serial loop ([RNG substreams](rng-substreams-and-parallel-determinism.md)).
Its measured share belongs to
[integrate thread scaling](../performance/integrate-thread-scaling.md)[^n31-i3].

**What the synthetic case shows, and does not.** On an integrand that is
exactly a 4:1 mixture of two channels' BW shapes, `α` moved
[0.5, 0.5] → [0.80, 0.20] in about two iterations, the shares `Wⱼ` equalised
(2.66e-2 vs 2.67e-2) and per-point variance fell ~1900× at equal cost. That
is the best case, near the zero-variance optimum; on a real `|M|²` with
continuum and interference the gain is smaller, and the test pins only the
direction and strict inequality. The combiner's own firing tests: strictly
below every single channel and below flat RAMBO on a multi-peak integrand, and
`overlapping_resonances_double_peak_resolved` (two nearby poles on one
invariant; dropping the second channel collapses its coverage ~1000×)[^n21-closeout].

## What MadEvent does instead

The MG5 loop-induced paper (§2.2) describes MadEvent's diagram-enhancement
method: the integral is split into channels, one per diagram topology, each
integrated with a parametrisation that undoes that topology's peaks. The
"RAMBO-style flat phase space × per-channel Jacobian" weight with optimum
`1/Σᵢ(1/Jᵢ)` that note 01's summary attributes to the paper's appendix is not
in the paper[^n01-loopind], and is not MadEvent's LO implementation either.
MadEvent integrates **one configuration per
`G<n>` directory**: `genps.f` maps the run's `iconfig` once
(`genps.f:681-684`), so every event a directory writes carries the same
configuration and there is no per-event channel draw[^mg-genps]. Under
`MULTI_CHANNEL` the matrix element is multiplied by `AMP2(j)/Σᵢ AMP2(i)`
(non-grouped output, `matrix_madevent_v4.inc:174-185`):[^mg-matrix]

```fortran
IF (MULTI_CHANNEL) THEN
    XTOT=0D0
    DO I=1,NDIAGS
        XTOT=XTOT+AMP2(I)
    ENDDO
```

a partition of unity over configurations built from per-diagram squared
amplitudes ([single-diagram enhancement](madevent-single-diagram-enhancement.md)).
Grouped output (`group_subprocesses`, on by default) forms the same ratio in
`matrix_madevent_group_v4.inc:213-228`, where `sde_strategy = 1` multiplies each
`AMP2` by `GET_CHANNEL_CUT(P, I)` and `sde_strategy = 2` replaces it with that
propagator factor.
Its channel phase space is the configuration's own map, never a mixture
density.

## The channel label: density share vs AMP2 share

Both decompositions partition the same integral and give the same σ, but they
label events differently:[^n27-b3]

| | P(label = j at x) |
|---|---|
| MadEvent's configuration | `AMP2ⱼ(x) / Σᵢ AMP2ᵢ(x)` — an amplitude share |
| our sampled channel | `αⱼ gⱼ(x) / g(x)` — a density share |

So our sampled channel is **not** the analogue of MadEvent's `ICONFIG`, and
nothing that must match MadEvent per event reads it. The event path draws a
configuration ∝ `AMP2` at the point, independent of the sampler, and
conditions the colour-flow draw on it (`AmplitudeEvaluator::select_config_and_flow`,
`helas/eval/compile.rs`; [colour and helicity selection](../events/colour-and-helicity-selection.md));
the clustering scale's configuration is drawn the same way
([configuration draw](../scales-pdf/clustering-configuration-draw.md)).

The difference is starkest where channel maps coincide. Channels whose
diagrams have only massless propagators and no spacelike line the cut-implied
floor can act on degenerate onto one map, and the channel index then carries no
information about the diagram: `gg_to_gg`'s s-channel and four-gluon channels
are bit-identical to each other. Measured while `uux_to_uux`'s two maps still
coincided this way (densities bit-identical, `α` frozen at uniform),
conditioning colour on the sampled channel moved its `ICOLUP` χ² from 1015 to
7268 on one degree of freedom (MadGraph writes flow 1 on 99.96% of events;
per-config σ 18.49 pb s-channel, 33 400 pb t-channel). Degenerate maps cost
variance, not correctness. The [spacelike floor](spacelike-floor.md)
differentiates the peripheral channels wherever the cuts imply one: with it,
`uux_to_uux`'s maps differ everywhere and `α` converges to
`[8.5e-6, 0.99999]`, and `gg_to_gg`'s goes to
`[3.2e-5, 3.2e-5, 0.496, 0.504]`[^n28-b2], so even distinct maps give a density-share
label unlike MadEvent's amplitude share. `g g > t t~` is the control: its top
pole makes the maps differ with no floor (worst pairwise density gap 0.84,
`α` = [0.267, 0.364, 0.369]).

Two adjacent facts from the same reading:[^n27-findings]

- Helicity pruning moves the incoherent per-diagram `AMP2` (39.5% on
  `gg_to_ttx`) while `|M|²` stays bit-exact, so `AMP2` comparisons must not
  assume pruning is neutral.
- MadGraph can merge several diagrams into one configuration, as with
  `e+ e- > e+ e-`'s γ and Z t-channel diagrams. `KNOWN_CONFIG_MERGE`
  (`tests/amplitude_oracle.rs`) lists every reference row where it does,
  coloured ones included (`ud_to_epemud_qcd0`: 21 accumulators over 35
  diagrams), and fails both ways: an unlisted row that merges and a listed row
  that does not[^oracle-merge] ([per-diagram AMP2](../amplitudes/per-diagram-amp2.md),
  [channel set](channel-set.md)).

Reference implementations read for this design: Sherpa `PHASIC++/Main/`
(multichannel integrator with separate colour and helicity integrators) and
POWHEG-BOX's `integrator.f` (MINT)[^n21-program]. The survey/refine "job
strategy" of the MG5 appendix is summarised in
[loop-induced MG5](../references/papers/loop-induced-madgraph5.md); how
points are spent per channel once `α` is set is
[budget allocation](channel-budget-allocation.md).
